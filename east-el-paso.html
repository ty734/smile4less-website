About our East El Paso office

The Montana Avenue office is where Smile 4 Less started, and it is built for families who cannot lose a day to an appointment. We are open from 7:00am to 9:30pm, which makes this the office people pick when school and work schedules leave no room.

We see families from across the east side: the neighborhoods along Montana, Zaragoza and Joe Battle, Montana Vista, Mission Ridge, Ysleta, and Northeast El Paso by way of Loop 375. Fort Bliss families are usually about 15 to 20 minutes away.

Good questions

East El Paso FAQ

Are you really open until 9:30pm?

Yes, Monday through Friday. Evening appointments are popular, so it helps to book ahead.

Do you offer a military discount?

Military and law enforcement families get 10% off treatment. Bring your ID and let us know when you book.

Can I start treatment the same day?

Usually, yes. If your free exam shows you're ready and you'd like to start, we can often place braces at the same visit.

Do you accept Medicaid?

Texas Medicaid does not cover orthodontic treatment, so we can't accept it for braces. Our in-house financing starts at $99 down.

Ready for a smile that fits your budget?

Your first visit is free, exam and X-ray included. We'll show you the plan and the price. No pressure, no surprises.
