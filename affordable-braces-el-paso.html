Cheap braces vs. affordable braces

"Cheap braces" gets searched a lot in El Paso, and we get why. Braces are a big expense for a family. But cheap and affordable aren't the same thing. Cheap usually means something got cut: the orthodontist, the follow-up, the retainers, or the office you can call when a bracket pops off. Affordable means the full treatment, done right, on a payment you can live with.

That second one is what Smile 4 Less is built for. Every plan is designed by a licensed orthodontist, every visit happens at a local office, and the monthly payment starts as low as $99.

What "$99 braces" actually means

  • $99 down to get started. Often the same day as your free consultation.
  • As low as $99 a month on our interest-free in-house financing. That's our lowest payment and many patients qualify; longer or more complex plans run higher. You'll see your exact number in writing before you decide.
  • Free first visit, exam and X-ray included.
  • No interest, no lender, no surprise fees. We finance it ourselves.
  • Most insurance accepted, and 10% off for military and law enforcement families.

Texas Medicaid does not cover braces, and that's a big part of why we built this plan. If you were counting on Medicaid, read this first.

Why affordable braces in El Paso used to mean a trip to Juarez

For a long time, the "affordable" option for El Paso families was across the bridge, and a lot of families still look into it. That's reasonable. What we set out to do is make staying local cost close to the same. With $99 down and payments as low as $99 a month, most families find the difference is a few dollars a month, and what they get back is 15 to 20 fewer border crossings over the course of treatment, no missed work for each adjustment, and an office minutes away when something breaks. Same great smile, a lot less driving.

What you get for the price

  • Treatment planned and supervised by a licensed orthodontist, not a general dentist or a mail-order kit
  • Metal braces, clear braces, or Invisalign, whichever fits your case and budget
  • Adjustment visits every 6 to 8 weeks at three El Paso offices, with the East office open until 9:30pm
  • A bilingual team. Se habla español.
  • Retainers and a written treatment plan so there's nothing hidden at the end

How to get your exact price

Book a free consultation. About 45 minutes. Exam, X-ray, a recommendation, and a written quote with your monthly payment. No pressure. Plenty of families start the same day; plenty take the paper home first. Both are fine.

Good questions

Affordable braces FAQ

Is Smile 4 Less the same as $99 Braces?

Yes. Smile 4 Less Orthodontics is the same practice El Paso knew as "$99 Braces," with the same $99-down financing. We changed the name because we do more than braces now (Invisalign, early treatment, retainers), but the price promise didn't change.

Are braces really $99 a month?

"As low as $99 a month" is our lowest in-house financing payment, and many patients qualify for it. Your payment depends on your treatment plan. We'll show you the exact number at your free visit before you commit.

Is there a catch?

No. The consultation is free, the financing is interest-free, and there are no hidden fees. The only thing that changes the number is your specific treatment plan.

What about cheap braces from a mail-order company?

Mail-order aligners skip the exam, the X-ray, and the orthodontist checking your progress. Moving teeth without knowing what's under the gums can cause real damage. Our pricing is often closer than people expect, and you get a specialist and a local office.

Can adults get the $99-down plan?

Yes. Kids, teens, and adults all qualify for in-house financing. More for adults.
