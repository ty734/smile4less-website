About our Horizon City office

Our Eastlake office serves Horizon City, Socorro, San Elizario, Clint, and the far east side. It opens at 7:00am, which parents love for before-school appointments, and it shares a building with a pediatric dental office, so a lot of our families are already down the hall.

We're proud Clint ISD partners in education. Se habla español. Military and law enforcement families get 10% off.

Good questions

Horizon City FAQ

How early can we come in?

The Horizon City office opens at 7:00am Monday through Friday, so before-school appointments are easy.

My kids see the pediatric dentist in the same building. Can you coordinate?

Yes. Many of our Horizon City families are referred from down the hall, and we work together on timing.

Do you offer Phase 1 (early) treatment here?

Yes. Expanders, space maintainers, and partial braces for younger kids, followed by Phase 2 if needed. More on early treatment.

Do you accept Medicaid?

Texas Medicaid does not cover orthodontic treatment, so we can't accept it for braces. Our in-house financing starts at $99 down.

Ready for a smile that fits your budget?

Your first visit is free, exam and X-ray included. We'll show you the plan and the price. No pressure, no surprises.
