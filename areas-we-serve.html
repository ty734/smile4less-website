Close to home, wherever home is

Three offices, spread across El Paso so nobody drives far: East on Montana, West on Remcon Circle, and Horizon City on Eastlake. Here's which office is closest to you, and roughly how long the drive is.

East El Paso

12244 Montana Ave, Bldg A Ste 400 · Mon–Fri 7am–9:30pm · (915) 304-3090

Closest for: Fort Bliss (main post and east side housing, about 15–20 min), Far East / Montana Vista, Mission Ridge, Eastside neighborhoods along Montana, Zaragoza and Joe Battle, Northeast El Paso via Loop 375 (about 20–25 min), Ysleta.

Open until 9:30pm, so it's the pick for after-shift and after-practice visits from anywhere in the county.

Fort Bliss families

Our East El Paso office on Montana is the closest to post and stays open until 9:30pm. Military and law enforcement families get 10% off treatment, we work with military dental plans, and if you PCS mid-treatment we'll transfer your records to your next orthodontist. Military families page →

Las Cruces and southern New Mexico

We don't have a Las Cruces office, but we do see families from Las Cruces, Anthony, and Sunland Park at our West El Paso location (about 45 minutes from Las Cruces via I-10). If the drive is worth it to you for the price, we'll make the visits quick. Every 6 to 8 weeks, about 20 to 30 minutes each. TODO (Tyler): confirm whether we want to actively market to Las Cruces

Coming from Juarez?

Plenty of our families have ties on both sides of the border, and some have started or finished treatment there. If you've moved, or you'd just rather not cross for every adjustment, we can take over care. Bring whatever records you have. New patient info →

Not sure which office?

Pick the one that's easiest. Your records work at all three, and if you need an evening visit at East while you normally go to Horizon City, that's fine.
