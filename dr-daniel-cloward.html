Dr. Daniel Cloward, DMD, MSD

Louisville-trained dentist with a master's in public health, Saint Louis University orthodontist, dad of five, and owner of a beagle named Scout. Horizon City patients call him "a wonderful orthodontist."

About Dr. Cloward

Dr. Cloward completed his bachelor's degree at Brigham Young University and earned a Master of Public Health from the University of Louisville before dental school. He then earned his dental degree from the University of Louisville, followed by two years of specialized orthodontic training at Saint Louis University.

He's committed to staying at the forefront of orthodontic care and regularly attends continuing-education courses. His public-health background shows up in how he practices: prevention-minded, plain-spoken, and focused on what actually helps families.
