How Invisalign works

Invisalign uses a series of clear plastic aligners, custom-made from a 3D scan of your teeth. Each set moves your teeth a tiny bit. You wear one set for about a week or two, then switch to the next. Over time, your teeth end up where they're supposed to be.

You take them out to eat, brush, and floss, then pop them back in. The catch: they only work when they're in, so plan on wearing them about 22 hours a day.

Who Invisalign is right for

  • Adults who want to fix their smile without anyone at work noticing. More for adults.
  • Teens who are responsible enough to keep them in (we'll be honest with you about this at the consult). More for teens.
  • Mild to moderate crowding, spacing, and bite issues. Bigger corrections often do better with braces, and we'll tell you if that's the case.

Invisalign is probably not the right fit if you know you'd forget to wear them, or if your bite needs the heavier lifting that braces do best. Not sure? That's exactly what the free visit is for.

What treatment looks like

  1. Free consultation. Exam and X-ray, and we'll talk about whether Invisalign is the right tool for your case.
  2. Digital scan. No goopy impressions. A quick scan builds your custom plan.
  3. Aligners arrive. You get several sets at a time, with clear instructions on when to switch.
  4. Check-ins every 6 to 10 weeks. Fast visits to make sure everything's tracking.
  5. Done. A retainer keeps your new smile in place.

How long does Invisalign take?

Many adult cases finish in 6 to 18 months. Teens and more involved cases can run longer. The single biggest factor is wear time: 22 hours a day keeps you on schedule.

Good questions

Invisalign FAQ

Is Invisalign more expensive than braces?

It can be a little more, depending on the case. Both are on our interest-free financing, and we'll show you the monthly payment for each option so you can decide.

Can teens really do Invisalign?

Many can. The teen version has small wear indicators so we (and you) can tell if they're being worn enough. We'll give you an honest recommendation at the consult.

Does Invisalign hurt?

You'll feel pressure for a day or two with each new set. Most patients describe it as "tight," not painful, and it fades quickly.

What about mail-order aligners?

Mail-order kits skip the exam, the X-ray, and the orthodontist checking your progress. Moving teeth without knowing what's happening under the gums can cause real problems. With us you get a specialist, a local office, and a price that's often closer than you'd think.

Can I eat with Invisalign in?

Take them out for anything other than water. That's actually the perk: no food restrictions at all.
