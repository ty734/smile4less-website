Colored braces

The fun part of braces

Metal braces come with tiny elastic bands around each bracket, and those come in every color. Pick one, pick two, do your school colors, go full rainbow. And you get to change them at every visit.

Schedule a Free ConsultationTry the color picker

How braces colors work

Each bracket is held to the wire by a small elastic tie (an "o-ring" or ligature). Those ties are what you're choosing when you pick braces colors. They're swapped out at every adjustment visit, usually every 6 to 8 weeks, so you're never stuck with a choice for long. Halloween orange in October, red and green in December, school colors for playoffs, whatever you want.

Colored ties are for metal braces. Clear braces use clear or tooth-colored ties to stay low-key, and Invisalign has no ties at all.

Which braces colors look best?

  • Want teeth to look whiter? Darker colors like navy, royal blue, purple, and dark green make teeth look brighter by contrast.
  • Want braces to blend in? Silver, light grey, or clear ties disappear against metal brackets. (Skip white; it can make teeth look yellow next to it.)
  • Skin tone tips: jewel tones (deep blue, emerald, purple, ruby) flatter most people. Bright pinks and turquoise pop on darker skin. Cool blues and purples suit lighter skin.
  • Colors that stain: clear and very light ties can pick up color from coffee, curry, and red sauce between visits. Darker colors hide it. We swap them out every visit anyway.
  • Colors to think twice about: yellow and brown can look like food. Black is bold but can look like a missing tooth from a distance. Some kids love it anyway.

Good questions

Braces colors FAQ

How often can I change my braces colors?

At every adjustment visit, usually every 6 to 8 weeks. Some patients pick a color for the season; some pick a new one every time.

Do colored braces cost more?

No. Colored ties are included with metal braces at no extra charge.

Can adults get colored braces?

Of course. Most adults choose silver or clear ties to keep things low-key, but it's your call.

Can I do a different color on each tooth?

Yes. Rainbow, alternating, or your own pattern. Just tell the assistant what you want.

Do the colors fade?

Light colors can tint a little from food and drinks over 6 to 8 weeks. They're replaced at each visit, so it never lasts.
